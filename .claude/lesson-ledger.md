# Lesson Ledger

Parking lot for transferable lessons that surface mid-task but are deliberately
**not** delivered in the moment, so teaching turns stay single-focus. Holds only
what's pending; delivered lessons live in `.claude/archive/lessons-delivered.md`.

## Protocol (for Claude)

- When a transferable lesson comes up during a correction or next-step turn:
  do NOT deliver it. Append one line under **Parked** and say only
  "(parked a lesson)" in the reply.
- Deliver parked items when Jackson reaches a natural pause (task complete,
  tests pass, end of a slice) or when he asks "what's in the ledger?"
- After delivering, move the entry to the archive file with the date.
- Entries are 1–2 lines: date — context — the lesson. No essays; the delivery
  conversation is where depth happens.
